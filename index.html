01 / what is a radical prototype?

A radical prototype is an experimental system built to challenge fundamental assumptions about how products, materials, and experiences are created.

rather than optimizing existing products, the lab builds working prototypes of alternative futures.

mission

Radical Prototypes creates physical and digital interventions that bring possible futures into the present, allowing new ideas, technologies, and ways of living to be experienced before they are widely adopted.

What is a radical prototype?
