Built Environment
& Energy Systems

Buildings that behave as instruments — sensing, generating, and conserving energy. We design facades that compute, envelopes that think, and infrastructures that close their own loops.

01.Athermal envelope

Energy leakage + insulation systems

High-resolution thermal mapping of building envelopes, coupled with adaptive insulation profiles that respond to seasonal and diurnal loads.

01.Bgenerative skin

Energy-generating facades

Photo-active, piezo-responsive cladding modules. Facades treated as distributed power plants — geometry tuned per solar exposure and wind incidence.

02 /

Advanced Materials
& Metamaterials

Geometry as mechanical property. We design lattices and microstructures whose behavior is tuned at the unit-cell level — for seating, footwear, aerospace, and beyond.

02.Alattice

3D printed lattice structures

Octet, gyroid, and re-entrant unit cells composed across gradient densities. Geometry that replaces material.

02.Btuning

Mechanical behavior tuning

Stiffness, damping, and energy absorption tuned spatially — anisotropic by design.

02.Capplication

Automotive seating + footwear systems

Pressure-mapped lattice midsoles and seat cores — replacing foam stacks with single-material printed assemblies.

02.Dauxetic

Auxetic surfaces + programmable matter

Surfaces with negative Poisson's ratio that thicken under tension. Matter with state — addressable, switchable, computationally reprogrammable.

Media Technology
& Memory Systems

Devices that record presence — not just images. We are building instruments for the capture, reconstruction, and replay of lived spatial experience.

Memory reconstruction

Photogrammetry + spatial reconstruction

03.D · experiential recording fps 240 · depth 14b

Experiential recording systems

Not video. Not VR. A new substrate for keeping moments — played back as space, not as frames.
